# PALS Database — Sprint 2 Implemented Schema

## subjects

- `id UUID` primary key, generated by the application
- `name varchar(255)` required and non-blank after trimming
- `description text` nullable
- `created_at timestamptz` required
- `updated_at timestamptz` required

## topics

- `id UUID` primary key, generated by the application
- `subject_id UUID` required FK to subjects, indexed
- `name varchar(255)` required and non-blank after trimming
- `description text` nullable
- `created_at timestamptz` required
- `updated_at timestamptz` required

## exams

- `id UUID` primary key, generated by the application
- `subject_id UUID` required FK to subjects
- `name varchar(255)` required and non-blank after trimming
- `exam_date timestamptz` required
- `description text` nullable
- `created_at timestamptz` required
- `updated_at timestamptz` required
- composite index on `(subject_id, exam_date)`

## exam_topics

- `exam_id UUID` FK to exams
- `topic_id UUID` FK to topics, indexed
- `weight numeric` required with `0 < weight <= 1`
- composite primary key `(exam_id, topic_id)`

## Sprint 1 deletion rules

- Subject to Topic: `ON DELETE RESTRICT`
- Subject to Exam: `ON DELETE RESTRICT`
- ExamTopic to Exam: `ON DELETE CASCADE`
- ExamTopic to Topic: `ON DELETE RESTRICT`

Subject and Topic restrictions are prechecked by services for friendly 409 responses. Deleting
an Exam deletes only its ExamTopic links and never deletes Topics.

Topic and Exam ownership is immutable through the Sprint 1 API. Exam/topic Subject equality is
an application-layer rule and intentionally has no database trigger. ExamTopic weights are not
required to sum to 1.

## questions

- `id UUID` primary key, generated by the application
- `topic_id UUID` required FK to topics with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`
- `prompt text` required and nonblank after trimming
- `answer_reference text` required and nonblank after trimming
- `difficulty varchar(10)` required: `easy`, `medium`, or `hard`
- `created_at timestamptz` required
- `updated_at timestamptz` required
- composite index on `(topic_id, created_at, id)`

Question Topic ownership is immutable. Questions may be changed or deleted only before their
first Attempt.

## attempts

- `id UUID` primary key, generated by the application
- `question_id UUID` required FK to questions with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`
- `correct boolean` required
- `hints_used integer` required with `0 <= hints_used <= 3`
- `solution_seen boolean` required
- `time_spent_seconds integer` required and nonnegative
- `created_at timestamptz` required
- composite index on `(question_id, created_at, id)`

Attempts are immutable historical evidence. Correctness is self-reported in Alpha.

## mastery

- `topic_id UUID` primary key and FK to topics with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`
- `score numeric(5,2)` required with `0 <= score <= 100`
- `updated_at timestamptz` required

Mastery is created lazily by the first successful Attempt. Existing Topics are not backfilled.
There is no cascade from Topic to Questions or Mastery, or from Question to Attempts.

## Sprint 2 transactional integrity

Attempt creation and Mastery mutation share one service-owned transaction. PostgreSQL row locks
serialize Mastery updates for a Topic; `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` safely handles two
simultaneous first Attempts.

## Sprint 3 planner projection

Sprint 3 adds no table, column, index, or migration. A Study Plan is calculated on demand from
`exams`, `exam_topics`, `topics`, optional `mastery`, and a captured UTC instant. Missing Mastery
is represented as `Decimal("0.00")` in memory and is not inserted. No priority or plan history is
stored.

## Deferred Alpha 0.1 schema

The following remain planned but are not part of Sprint 3:

- Topic hierarchy (`parent_topic_id`) and importance
- Exam target and maximum scores
- Study sessions
- AI interaction logging

Later integrity rules, including mastery ranges and transactional attempt/mastery updates, are
deferred with those tables.
